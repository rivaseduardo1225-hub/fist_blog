Comparison 2

Historical emissions responsibility

The countries with the least responsibility for the crisis are often the most exposed to it.

Cumulative CO₂ since 1850 · schematic

Majority of cumulative CO₂ since 1850 · today's wealthy nations <0.4%

What this means: the Philippines produces under 0.4% of global CO₂ while the majority of cumulative emissions since 1850 come from today's wealthy nations (IPCC, 2023; La Viña et al., 2024). The bar illustrates the qualitative gap and is not drawn to an exact scale. This is the basis of the climate-justice case for the Loss and Damage Fund.

Comparison 3

Livelihood exposure

How much a country's workforce is exposed to weather by the nature of its work.

Exposure profile · qualitative

Livelihood exposure by type of work, with a qualitative level and source.
Work typeExposureWhy
Outdoor dependence (farming, fishing) High Subsistence and smallholder work is directly exposed to storms and season shifts.
Indoor insulation (service, technology) Low Service and technology work is comparatively insulated from weather events.

Thomas et al. (2021): the same hazard produces different outcomes depending on whether a livelihood depends on the weather. The Philippines has a relatively high share of outdoor, weather-exposed work.
