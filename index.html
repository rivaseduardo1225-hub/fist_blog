Beyond rising temperatures: why climate change is a human crisis

The Philippines sits inside the typhoon belt, faces the world's highest disaster risk, and produces under 0.4% of global emissions. This investigation follows the risk from the atmosphere down to the household — and asks who carries it, and who pays for it.
