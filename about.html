About this report

Terrapulse STS is a class project, and it reads like one on purpose. BSIT 2-05, Group 2 · Submitted September 2026.

Research, not marketing

Why this investigation exists

We took a Science, Technology & Society paper and turned its findings into something a reader can check, compare and argue with — a summary of the literature, a comparison of national capacities, and a short self-assessment of local preparedness.

Our subject is climate change treated as both an environmental and a social crisis, with the Philippines as the focus: climate justice, disaster preparedness, economic inequality, displacement and sustainable technology.

We did not gather new field data. Every claim here traces back to the published sources in Chapters I–III, listed in full below. Where a claim could not be verified from the source material, it is flagged rather than presented as fact.

Method & limits

How to read the evidence

  • Primary source. The report is built from the group's research document, "Beyond Rising Temperatures: Climate Change as an Environmental and Social Crisis."
  • Statistics. Each figure is paired with its source and reporting year. Historical figures are labelled as historical, not current.
  • Schematic charts. The bars on the climate-data page are illustrative, derived from the qualitative Chapter II table, and are not measured values.
  • Citations. In-text citations follow APA 7th; the full list appears below.
  • Flags. Any statement that cannot be verified from the source is marked "[Flag for review]" instead of being asserted.
  • Photography. All images are illustrative stock photographs credited to Unsplash — they document no specific event in this report.
